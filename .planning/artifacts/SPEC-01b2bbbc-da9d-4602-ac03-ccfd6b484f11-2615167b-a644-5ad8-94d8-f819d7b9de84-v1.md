## 7. Concrete interaction-state contracts

All quantitative thresholds, durations, rates, caps, scores, and initial values are loaded through an explicitly versioned proposed profile. A test may reproduce the PRD’s current profile as a fixture; passing that fixture does not accept the profile. The stable acceptance target is the semantic shape and safety invariants below.

### 7.1 Shared runtime contract

- One serialized simulation owner accepts ordered commands/events and emits immutable semantic snapshots, presentation intents, and bounded diagnostics.
- Every interaction carries stable session/gesture/portion/object/behavior IDs as applicable. Every owned gesture has exactly one terminal result. Every consequential marker is idempotent.
- Rendering and playback may report lag but cannot award affection, nutrition, familiarity, attention credit, object edits, or movement completion.
- Owner lifecycle commands preempt; environment invalidation follows; direct interaction follows; valid committed behavior follows; autonomous competition is last.
- Hidden/paused input does not advance ordinary needs or learning. Quiet/Focus suppress directed bids. Transition from effectively unavailable to available starts the configured grace once; redundant toggles or a remaining restriction cannot restart or bypass it.
- A deterministic fixture record contains version identity, seed, ordered bounded inputs and return events. Validation completes before any prefix executes.

### 7.2 Affection and grooming

State graph: outside care → notice → accept or boundary → lean/enjoy → plateau → warning/boundary → settle. Cancellation may leave any owned phase through its permitted safe exit; Hide/Pause/Quit is immediate at the presentation boundary.

Inputs: deliberate local contact samples with body-relative region, elapsed contact time and quality; brief touch; pause; gesture end/cancel; control/environment preemption.

Owned state: contact desire, satisfaction, overstimulation, social appetite, receptiveness and the current interaction/session identity. A brief interruption preserves the accumulated state; reopening Pet does not reset satisfaction or stimulation.

Invariants:

- One valid brief contact produces acknowledgment and may end without asking for more.
- Gentle receptive contact can become more welcome before saturation makes the desire fall; the arc must end naturally.
- Rough/impossible input lowers or earns no quality and may end contact, but never labels abuse, mutates the real pointer, synthesizes input, or creates relationship loss.
- Warning posture precedes withdrawal when presentation time permits. An unreceptive fixture may acknowledge and decline immediately.
- Still contact cannot mint repeated stroke or familiarity credit. Familiarity is awarded, if at all, once by a separate bounded completed-session rule.
- Grooming uses the same consent/boundary path with its own content definition; it is not an indefinite automatic session.

Evidence: FND-01 deterministic curves; S1-CORE gesture/session tests; S1-CARE pointer/keyboard/VoiceOver; S1-ILLUSION live boundary legibility; scenarios B/C/I/K.

### 7.3 Interactive and autonomous play

State graph: invitation/discovery → orient/watch → stalk → pursue → catch or miss → recovery → renew or disengage. Target loss may enter one bounded search before disengagement. A committed leap finishes only to a still-valid landing or recovery.

Inputs/state: toy identity, local anchor/pattern features, energy, play motivation, arousal, overstimulation, sleep pressure, per-toy boredom/novelty/preference, route/target generation and session identity.

Invariants:

- Interest can wane through fatigue, boredom, satisfaction or overstimulation; session reopening and toy switching cannot erase global fatigue.
- Slow movement, dart, pause and disappearance affect opportunity; owner input never guarantees a catch.
- Catch/effect commits only at a verified semantic contact marker. Miss/search/release cannot double-commit.
- Mouse lure stays in owned play space and loses capture safely. Laser does not move the real pointer and ends with a catchable non-food closure by default; food remains optional.
- Loose-toy identity/location persists through bat/carry/hide/rediscovery; recover-toys changes placement, not novelty/preference.

Evidence: FND-01 retained-fatigue replay; S2-CARE seeded lure and human trajectories; S3-WORLD loose-toy/object routes; S4-DEPTH laser/content; D/G/I/X1/X7.

### 7.4 Meals, treats and water

State graph: offer/replace → notice → approach → sniff → accept, exploratory bite or refuse → bite/pause loop → lick/groom → settle. Water uses a short autonomous visit, not a depletion chore.

Identity/state: stable portion ID, bite ordinal, bowl/object ID, food definition, remaining quantity, appetite/satiety, stable taste/texture affinity, familiarity and temporary repetition. Nutrition commits only for consumed fractions at accepted bite markers.

Invariants:

- Repeated commands/renderer callbacks cannot duplicate a portion or bite. Replacing while eating finishes or cancels at the safe current-bite boundary.
- Basic has a safe acceptance path when appetite permits; a full cat may still decline. Refusal of another flavor causes no penalty or stable dislike by itself.
- An exploratory bite commits only its fraction. Continue/leave is reevaluated from semantic state.
- Treat effects diminish and cannot create unbounded familiarity. Water absence creates no harm, debt or new care loop.
- Automatic Basic availability, flavor seeds and all thresholds remain D-07/profile decisions, not accepted by this plan.

Evidence: existing repeated-command regression; S2-CARE portion/bite ledger and controlled food fixtures; S2-STORE partial-meal restart; S4-DEPTH treats/water; F/J/L/X1.

### 7.5 Directed attention

State graph: ineligible/quiet → eligible after grace → approach/initial invitation → wait → optional single stronger invitation → acknowledged, unanswered or cancelled → retreat/rest → cooldown.

Invariants:

- The entire directed sequence consumes one persisted availability-time token; passive gaze, breathing and nearby rest are not bids and cannot hide repeated nudging.
- Quiet, Focus, Hide, Pause, sleep and lock do not advance availability time. Leaving a restriction starts grace and creates no queued bid.
- Silence never decreases familiarity or creates escalation/debt. Unanswered completion retreats and starts the persisted cooldown.
- Owner controls preempt every bid phase. Acknowledgment does not refund a token or erase another need.
- Food/play/contact/proximity presentations must be distinguishable without sound before text is used to compensate.

Evidence: FND-01 grace regression; S2-CARE randomized/restart/toggle budget properties; S4-DEPTH silent observer study; E/K/X1.

### 7.6 Habitat, placement and environmental recovery

State graph: immutable world generation → eligible support/route → reservation → approach/takeoff → revalidate each motion step → land/use → release. Invalidation moves to physical exit/replan or explicit visible recovery; it never leaves Sterling on stale geometry.

Object-edit transaction: preview → validate footprint/clearance/exclusions → if occupied reserve safe exit → exit or explicit recovery → commit expected generation → release old support. Cancel leaves the prior object/world unchanged. Replacing/removing the home requires a valid replacement.

Invariants:

- Behavior uses typed affordances, not menu labels. Only app-owned objects are launch dependencies.
- Display-local points/scales are converted at the host boundary; stale external/native tokens never persist.
- Passive visuals ignore input. Placement/care input is deliberate, bounded and accessible.
- Virtual folder theatre reads no folder contents. Optional ledges, real icon anchor and external masking remain unavailable unless their separate capability gates pass.
- Display loss, Space change, support movement/close, permission revocation and exclusion changes cancel stale plans before another movement effect.

Evidence: S0-DESKTOP physical matrix; S3-WORLD property/phase invalidation; S3-PLACE keyboard/VoiceOver transaction tests; G/H/I/L/X4/X7.

### 7.7 Continuity, time and reset

Modes: visible/available, Quiet/Focus, Hidden/Paused, fully obscured, asleep/locked/closed, and clock/timezone change each have explicit clock semantics. Runtime timers use an injected monotonic clock; wall time is bounded return/routine evidence only.

Persistence contract: versioned semantic snapshot, content/tuning/app identity, RNG checkpoint, bounded budgets/history/evidence, habitat objects, food ledger and semantic resume intent; atomic primary plus previous-good generation. Native windows, renderer nodes, external IDs and gesture ownership never persist.

Invariants:

- Absence creates no hunger debt, relationship loss, missed-event replay, catch-up meal, duplicated reward or budget refill.
- Long return clamps transient needs into a comfortable recoverable range without changing stable familiarity/tastes; the exact values remain proposed profile data.
- Interrupted meals resume only from committed bites. Airborne motion and external support do not resume blindly.
- Corrupt/incompatible saves never overwrite the recoverable originals. Settings, layout and memories have separate explicit reset consequences; cancel mutates nothing.
- Determinism is same-version semantic reproducibility, not a promise of cross-version/GPU/platform bit identity.

Evidence: FND-01 fixture replay; S2-STORE migration/corruption/disk/clock/100-cycle tests; J/L/X1/X3/X5.
