# sterling.io

A little life on your Mac: one independent local companion named in honor of the founder’s late cat. Quiet presence, reciprocal affection, play that wanes, feeding/preferences, bounded invitations, habitat and forgiving persistent familiarity remain the product promise.

The accepted PRD v1 is the frozen planning baseline. The current code is an S0 feasibility prototype. The production-foundation drafts make the existing scope executable without accepting new defaults, likeness, product design, phase exit or release.

The owner has selected public native GitHub delivery and Developer ID/notarization/Sparkle operations; product proof remains separate. Keep the one-owner pure simulation and passive input-safe presentation boundaries. Do not invent the real Sterling’s appearance or history.
