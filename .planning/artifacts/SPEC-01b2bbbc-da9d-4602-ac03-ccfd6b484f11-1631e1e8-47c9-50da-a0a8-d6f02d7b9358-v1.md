## 4. D-01 model-sheet deliverable and acceptance

### 4.1 Deliverable

The character artist prepares a versioned, source-traceable model-sheet candidate linked to the qualified references. The packet discloses the authoring tools/services used and which references each processed. It includes:

- front, unmirrored left/right profile, left/right three-quarter, and rear views;
- face, eye, ear, nose/muzzle, paw, and tail callouts;
- body proportions and characteristic posture/gait notes;
- a left/right coat-and-marking map with asymmetries called out explicitly;
- neutral sRGB palette swatches and one documented neutral lighting direction;
- standing, walking, sitting, rest/loaf, affection invitation, and early-boundary silhouettes;
- founder-confirmed temperament/mannerism notes, each source-tagged; and
- an uncertainty layer listing every unsourced or deliberately deferred detail.

The sheet may use disclosed assisted-authoring methods that fit the founder's actual permissions. Prototype geometry and generic/non-reference placeholders may support construction or pipeline exploration, but they cannot establish Sterling-specific facts or likeness. A reference-based candidate can be submitted for review; only the founder's approval of the exact version resolves D-01. Generic anatomical reference must be separately traceable and distinguished from Sterling-specific evidence.

### 4.2 D-01 acceptance criteria

D-01 may be recorded as resolved only when the founder reviews one exact model-sheet version and the receipt states, separately:

- likeness: accept / revise / defer;
- coat, markings, and asymmetry: accept / revise / defer;
- anatomy, proportions, posture, and gait: accept / revise / defer;
- personal temperament and mannerisms: accept / revise / defer; and
- voice: accept, explicitly defer, or declare out of current scope.

Additional acceptance conditions:

- every Sterling-specific claim traces to a qualified reference or explicit founder statement;
- left/right markings are not silently mirrored;
- disputed or low-confidence details remain labeled rather than averaged into a guess;
- the exact model-sheet files and review renderings have hashes;
- founder corrections are incorporated into a new version, not overwritten in place; and
- the receipt names what is **not** approved. Voice deferral, for example, permits no claim of Sterling-specific vocal likeness.

D-01 approves the likeness/trait record only. It does not approve the layered 2.5D pipeline, runtime performance, direct touch, platform support, or launch art scope.

## 5. 80/160/240-point desktop study

After an exact D-01 model sheet is accepted, the artist and rendering engineer create a bounded study from that sheet. Offline-authored layered 2.5D remains the **proposed** treatment until D-05.

Test each size at actual logical-point dimensions against app-owned light, dark, and visually busy fixtures; do not capture a private desktop. Show unmirrored left/right facings and both still and motion review loops.

| Size | Acceptance focus |
|---|---|
| 80 pt | Sterling remains identifiable from silhouette, proportions, major markings, facing, and tail/ear posture. Paw contact and the contact shadow remain coherent. Fine facial detail is not allowed to carry the only meaning. |
| 160 pt | Proposed default-size study: likeness, gaze, independent ear/tail cues, breath, anticipation, planted contacts, head acknowledgment, affection lean, and early withdrawal remain legible without halos or visual chatter. |
| 240 pt | Enlarged study preserves anatomy, markings, masks, edges, layer alignment, and lighting without exposing rig seams or making the character feel like a different model. |
| All sizes | No asymmetric marking is mirrored; no incompatible whole-body crossfade creates a double silhouette; reduced-motion alternatives preserve blink, breath, acceptance, and withdrawal; light/dark/busy fixtures do not erase the primary posture cue. |

The review board contains the same bounded motion set at all sizes:

- neutral rest plus breathing;
- walk, turn, and stop with planted contacts;
- sit/neutral;
- brief head acknowledgment;
- lean or chin lift;
- early boundary cue and leave; and
- safe transition toward the nook/home pose.

A generic or technical synthetic/non-reference placeholder may test the exporter, renderer, or validator. Label it **technical fixture only**: it cannot prove likeness, resolve D-01, or count as production-asset acceptance. It may inform bounded technical parts of D-05/FZ-04 but cannot close them as production proof. A source-traceable, reference-based candidate may be reviewed for D-01/D-05 when its source use and authoring tools are disclosed, applicable rights are documented, and the founder reviews the exact version.

## 6. Minimal initial asset-manifest acceptance

The manifest minimum is an evidence minimum, not a launch inventory or art budget.

### 6.1 Pack-level record

`CharacterPackManifest v1` records:

- `schemaVersion`, `packID`, `characterID`;
- the exact `likenessApprovalID` from D-01;
- `rigVersion`, `modelSheetVersion`, and `contentVersion`;
- source checksums and rights/license provenance;
- color profile, logical body length, and supported facings;
- clip, region-map, atlas-page, and audio-cue indexes;
- build-tool version and pack checksum.

An absent `likenessApprovalID` means **non-likeness fixture only**.

### 6.2 Clip-level record

Every `AnimationClipManifest v1` entry records:

- clip ID/version and rig version;
- duration, loop mode, and authored facing;
- entry pose, exit pose, and compatible predecessors;
- root displacement and planted contacts;
- ordered semantic markers and safe-exit markers;
- sockets, body-region map, occlusion masks, and allowed additive channels;
- reduced-motion alternative;
- behavior tags and atlas references; and
- source/export checksum and provenance link.

Renderer callbacks and animation markers are presentation facts; they never award affection, meals, familiarity, or other simulation effects.

### 6.3 Minimum clip graph

The first accepted graph contains the review-board motions above and a valid safe path from every clip to neutral rest or home recovery. After that core graph passes, the S0-ART render spike may add one bounded lure orient/stalk/pounce/recover chain to exercise root motion, contact, interruption, and safe exit. That addendum does not approve the production play inventory or tuning.

Manifest acceptance requires:

- all required IDs and checksums are real exporter outputs, not guessed placeholders;
- the exact pack validates with no duplicate IDs, invalid timing/order, missing contacts, incompatible poses/rigs, missing safe exits, atlas-bounds errors, or required reduced-motion gaps;
- every authored facing and asymmetry matches the accepted D-01 sheet;
- body-region maps are used only inside the explicit care surface unless D-02/FZ-01 later approves another input policy;
- entry/exit/contact behavior is visually reviewed at 80, 160, and 240 points;
- provenance and rights are complete for every source and production asset; and
- technical and resource results identify the actual build, pack, device, OS, method, and limitations.

The study may inform FZ-04, but brief runs do not prove sustained resource budgets. Tests on a candidate device do not settle D-06 support.
